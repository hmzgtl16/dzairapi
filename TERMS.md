## Free Public API

DzairAPI is free to use. The service is provided to make Algerian administrative-division data easier to access for developers, researchers, and public-interest projects. The API covers provinces (wilayas), districts (dairas), and municipalities (communes) with localized names in Arabic and French.

There is no paid service-level agreement, uptime guarantee, or support guarantee.

## Rate Limits and Blocking

Rate limits are applied to protect the service. The default limit is **10 requests per 10 seconds per IP address**, enforced by Bucket4j with a Caffeine cache backend. Excessive, harmful, or abusive traffic may be throttled, blocked, or otherwise restricted.

The limits are documented in [README.md](./README.md#rate-limiting), but may change when needed to protect reliability.

## Data Accuracy and Sources

The administrative-division data in DzairAPI is compiled from texts published in the Official Journal of the Algerian Republic (Journal Officiel de la République Algérienne Démocratique et Populaire). This includes province (wilaya) codes, Arabic and French names. URL slugs are generated by the project and are not part of the official publication.

DzairAPI is an independent project. It is not an official publication, and the compiled data is provided for convenience. For legal or critical use, verify the data against the Official Journal.

The data is provided for convenience and should be independently verified before use in critical systems, legal workflows, emergency services, financial decisions, or other high-impact contexts.
